EXPERIMENT 4: DONUT CHART
Aim: Visualize the distribution of transactions by payment method.

Power BI steps:
1. Import payment_methods.csv.
2. Select the Donut chart visual.
3. Drag Payment_Method to Legend and Transactions to Values.
4. Turn on detail labels in the Format visual pane.
5. Set the title to "Transactions by Payment Method".
6. Save as DV_Experiment_4_Donut_Chart.pbix.

Expected result: A donut chart comparing transaction counts by payment method.
