EXPERIMENT 1: LINE CHART
Aim: Create a line chart to show the monthly sales trend.

Power BI steps:
1. Open Power BI Desktop.
2. Select Home > Get data > Text/CSV and import monthly_sales.csv.
3. In Report view, select the Line chart visual.
4. Drag Month to X-axis and Sales to Y-axis (Values).
5. Set the title to "Monthly Sales Trend".
6. Save as DV_Experiment_1_Line_Chart.pbix.

Expected result: A line chart showing the change in sales from January to December.
