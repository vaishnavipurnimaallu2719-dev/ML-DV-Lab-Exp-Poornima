EXPERIMENT 7: CARDS AND KPI
Aim: Display key performance indicators such as total sales and target achievement.

Power BI steps:
1. Import monthly_targets.csv.
2. In the Fields pane, right-click the table and choose New measure.
3. Create the measures shown in DAX_Measures.txt.
4. Add a Card visual and place Total Sales in it.
5. Add another Card visual and place Total Target in it.
6. Add a KPI visual. Set Indicator to Total Sales, Trend axis to Month and Target goals to Total Target.
7. Save as DV_Experiment_7_Cards_KPI.pbix.

Expected result: Cards display actual and target totals; KPI visual compares performance with the target.
