EXPERIMENT 2: BAR CHART
Aim: Compare units sold across products.

Power BI steps:
1. Import product_sales.csv using Home > Get data > Text/CSV.
2. Select the Clustered bar chart visual.
3. Drag Product to Y-axis and Units_Sold to X-axis (Values).
4. Sort by Units_Sold in descending order.
5. Set the title to "Units Sold by Product".
6. Save as DV_Experiment_2_Bar_Chart.pbix.

Expected result: A horizontal bar chart comparing the units sold for each product.
