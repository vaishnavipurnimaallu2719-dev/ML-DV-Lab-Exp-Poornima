EXPERIMENT 3: PIE CHART
Aim: Show each category's share of total revenue.

Power BI steps:
1. Import category_revenue.csv.
2. Select the Pie chart visual.
3. Drag Category to Legend and Revenue to Values.
4. In Format visual > Detail labels, turn labels on and choose percent of total if available.
5. Set the title to "Revenue Share by Category".
6. Save as DV_Experiment_3_Pie_Chart.pbix.

Expected result: A pie chart showing the proportion of revenue contributed by each category.
