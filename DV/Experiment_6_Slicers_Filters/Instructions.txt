EXPERIMENT 6: SLICERS AND FILTERS
Aim: Filter report data interactively using slicers.

Power BI steps:
1. Import employee_performance.csv.
2. Add a Table visual with Employee, Department, City and Performance_Score.
3. Add a Slicer visual and drag Department into its Field.
4. Add a second Slicer and drag City into its Field.
5. Select different departments and cities; confirm the table responds.
6. Save as DV_Experiment_6_Slicers_Filters.pbix.

Expected result: Selecting a department or city filters the employee table.
